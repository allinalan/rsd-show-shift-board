The last sync from the Sheet

A sync carries what changed on the Sheet since the last one: shifts added, removed and re-staffed, shift rows, new events, event details. Where the board was edited too, the board wins and the item is listed here. Booking status comes from VectorConnect and promoter contacts are edited on the board, so neither comes from the Sheet.

Sign in to edit

Only people on the editor list can change the board. Enter your email and we'll send you a sign-in code — no password. Type the code in here and this device stays signed in until you sign out.

On iPhone, sign in from the Home Screen icon instead of Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen, open the icon, sign in there. Safari forgets a sign-in after a week away; the icon doesn't.

Roll 2026 forward into 2027

Every event moves ahead one year to the same weekend. Status resets to Prospective, shifts are cleared, VC numbers are blanked. Promoter info, tier, access, cost, best CPO and booth structure carry over. Dates are marked est. until you set them. Events already rolled are skipped, so this is safe to run again.
